COMPASS Framework

Is Your EP Candidate COMPASS Ready?

COMPASS (Complementarity Assessment Framework) was introduced by MOM in September 2023 and applies to all new Employment Pass applications. Candidates must score a minimum of 40 points across five criteria: salary benchmarked against peers in the same sector and occupation (C1), qualifications assessed against the Singapore Qualifications Framework (C2), diversity of the company's local PMET workforce (C3), support for skills development through SkillsFuture (C4), and company track record on local employment (C5). Our COMPASS verification covers all five criteria to give your EP applications the strongest possible compliance profile.

C1

Salary

Does the candidate earn at or above the 50th percentile for their occupation and sector? Earning above the 75th percentile gives bonus points.

C2

Qualifications

Does the candidate hold a degree or higher qualification recognised by MOM? Top-tier university graduates receive bonus points.

C3

Diversity

Does hiring this candidate improve your company's local PMET nationality diversity? High concentration of a single nationality reduces points.

C4

SkillsFuture

Has your company supported local employee skills development? Active SkillsFuture participation earns bonus points.

C5

Local Employment

Does your company have a strong track record of employing Singaporean PMETs? Companies on the FCF watchlist score zero on this criterion.

Minimum 40 Points

Candidates must score at least 40 points to qualify. Our verification covers all five criteria to maximise your EP application success rate.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Background Checks and Work Pass Compliance in Singapore

Is background screening mandatory in Singapore?
Background screening is not universally mandatory under Singapore law but is required for specific regulated sectors. MAS-regulated financial institutions must screen all staff. MOH-registered healthcare providers must screen clinical staff. CAAS requires aviation security personnel to be screened. MOM requires work pass verification for all foreign hires. Most Singapore employers also conduct voluntary screening under the MOM Fair Consideration Framework and PDPA guidelines.
What is the MOM Fair Consideration Framework?
The Fair Consideration Framework requires all Singapore employers to fairly consider Singaporeans for job opportunities before hiring foreigners on EP or S-Pass. Employers with 10 or more employees must advertise qualifying jobs on MyCareersFuture for at least 14 calendar days before submitting a work pass application. Employers with discriminatory hiring practices can be placed on the FCF watchlist and face restrictions on all future work pass applications.
What is COMPASS and how does it affect EP applications?
COMPASS is a points-based system introduced by MOM in September 2023 for all new Employment Pass applications. Candidates must score at least 40 points across salary benchmarking, qualifications, workforce diversity, SkillsFuture support, and company local employment track record. We verify all five COMPASS criteria to give your EP applications the strongest possible compliance profile.
What is the PDPA and how does it apply to background checks?
The Personal Data Protection Act requires employers to obtain written candidate consent before collecting personal data, notify candidates of the purpose, use data only for the stated purpose, store it securely, and not retain it longer than necessary. Organisations that breach PDPA can face financial penalties of up to S$1 million or 10% of annual Singapore turnover.
What is the difference between EP, S-Pass, and Work Permit?
Employment Pass is for foreign professionals, managers, and executives earning at least S$5,000 per month who meet COMPASS criteria. S-Pass is for mid-level skilled staff earning at least S$3,150 per month with relevant qualifications, subject to sector-specific quota limits. Work Permit is for semi-skilled foreign workers in construction, marine, manufacturing, and services sectors from MOM-approved source countries.
What is CPF and why does it need to be verified?
The Central Provident Fund is Singapore's mandatory social security savings scheme for Citizens and Permanent Residents. Employers must make monthly CPF contributions for all eligible employees. CPF verification confirms that a candidate's declared employment history is accurate and that previous employers made required contributions, protecting you from hiring candidates who misrepresent their work history.
What is SingPass and how is it used in background screening?
SingPass is Singapore's national digital identity system. In background screening, SingPass verification confirms a candidate's identity, NRIC number, residency status, and work pass details with high authenticity. Candidates consent to share their SingPass data directly, reducing document fraud risk significantly compared to manual document verification.
What is CBS and when is a credit check required?
CBS stands for Credit Bureau Singapore. Credit checks are required for roles involving financial decision-making, access to company funds, fiduciary duties, or sensitive financial data. MAS-regulated institutions must assess financial integrity for regulated functions. A CBS report shows credit score, outstanding credit facilities, repayment history, and any bankruptcy or litigation records.
What is ACRA verification?
ACRA is the Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority of Singapore. An ACRA BizFile search reveals a company's registered directors, shareholders, paid-up capital, and filing history. ACRA verification confirms a candidate's declared employment with Singapore-registered companies and identifies any undisclosed directorships or deregistered companies in their employment history.
How long does background screening take in Singapore?
SingPass identity verification: instant. MOM Work Pass status check: instant to 24 hours. CBS credit check: same day to 24 hours. ACRA corporate verification: same day. Standard employment and education verification: 1 to 3 business days. International screening and overseas police clearance: 5 to 15 business days depending on the source country. Our standard Singapore background check package averages 24 hours.
