06Security & Compliance

Built for UAE data protection and regulatory expectations.

The specifics your compliance team needs before signing — stated plainly.

Every check runs on audited infrastructure, with consent and audit trails captured automatically.

  • Encryption: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2+ in transit
  • Access controls: role-based permissions with SSO; immutable audit logs on every action
  • Retention: configurable — candidate data deleted after 12 months by default
