How long does background verification take in Nigeria?

Identity checks are instant to 24 hours through NIMC NIN lookup and BVN validation. Address checks take 24 to 72 hours, employment checks 3 to 10 business days, education checks 5 to 10 business days (including NYSC discharge certificate verification), Police Clearance Certificate from the NPF 1 to 5 business days, and court record checks 1 to 3 business days.

What documents are needed for background verification in Nigeria?

Usually a government ID such as a NIN slip or National e-ID card, Nigerian passport, FRSC driver's licence, or INEC Permanent Voter's Card (PVC); a Bank Verification Number (BVN); education certificates, transcripts and the NYSC discharge certificate; previous employment letters and payslips; and address proof. The candidate also gives digital consent, which Verifitech captures through OTP, as required under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Is background verification legal in Nigeria?

Yes. Nigerian employers can verify candidates with informed, documented consent under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), overseen by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). Verifitech captures consent for every check and follows the Act's lawful-processing, purpose-limitation and data-minimization principles. Get your own legal advice on policies specific to your industry.
