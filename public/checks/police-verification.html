Overview

What is Police Verification in India?

Quick answer

Police verification is the process by which a local police station confirms a person's identity and address and checks their details against local crime records and CCTNS-linked State Police databases. The outcome is either a police verification report — used by employers and landlords — or a formal Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) stating that the person has no adverse criminal record.

In India, police verification is requested during pre-employment background screening, before renting out property, while hiring domestic help or drivers, by security agencies that must verify guards under the PSARA framework, and by passport authorities before issuing certain passports and PCCs. The verification is handled by the jurisdictional police station: a beat officer confirms residence, checks station records and, where available, State Police citizen portals connected through CCTNS (Crime and Criminal Tracking Network and Systems).

There are two ways to complete it. Digital police verification runs through State Police portals (Delhi, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Telangana and others) and returns a digitally signed, QR-verifiable certificate, often pushed directly to the candidate's DigiLocker. Physical verification is completed at the police station with a stamped character or verification certificate. Passport-related police verification follows the Pre-PV, Post-PV and No-PV modes defined by Passport Seva, and DigiLocker-issued documents carry the same legal validity as physical ones (DigiLocker). For a complete screening programme, police verification is usually combined with employment verification, address verification and a police clearance certificate check — see our guide to background verification companies in India.

Know the difference

Police Verification vs Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)

The two terms are often used interchangeably, but they are not the same thing. Here is how they compare.

AspectPolice VerificationPolice Clearance Certificate (PCC)
What it is A process — the local police station verifies identity, current and permanent address, and checks for criminal history at the local level. A document — an official certificate issued by the police stating there are no criminal convictions or pending cases.
Typically requested by Employers, landlords, security agencies and households hiring domestic help. Passport offices, embassies and immigration authorities for visas, immigration and long-term stays.
Output Verification report or character certificate issued through the police station. Formal PCC with SP/DCP office stamp — physical or digital via DigiLocker with QR verification.
Typical validity Employee reports are valid as on the verification date (annual re-verification advised for security staff and drivers); tenant certificates usually match the lease duration. 6 months for immigration/visa purposes and up to 1 year for employment, depending on the requesting authority.

For tenant screening, landlords typically reject applicants with violent crime history or property-related offenses.
