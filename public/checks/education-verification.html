Overview

What is Education Verification?

Quick answer

Education verification confirms that a qualification — degree, diploma, school record or professional certification — was actually awarded to the candidate by a legitimate institution. Verifitech checks directly with registrars, boards and licensing bodies, screens for diploma mills and forged certificates, and returns an audit-ready report.

A degree is the single most commonly inflated claim on a resume — and the easiest to fabricate, from edited PDFs to full diploma mills. Our checks go to the source: university and college registrars in India, the National Student Clearinghouse in the US, MOE equivalency channels in the UAE, polytechnic and university records in Singapore, and QQI-validated bodies in Ireland. Institution legitimacy, award details, dates and the candidate’s name on the record are all confirmed independently.

Education checks sit naturally beside the rest of the stack: ID verification confirms who the certificate belongs to, employment verification covers the work history around it, and court record checks complete the picture for regulated roles. How Education Verification Works

From declared credentials to a tamper-proof report — a transparent, consented flow.

1

Credential Declaration

The candidate lists each qualification with institution, course, dates and certificate numbers, and consents to verification.

2

Institution Legitimacy Screen

Each institution is validated against official registers and diploma-mill indicators before any outreach.

3

Source Verification

Registrars, clearinghouses, boards and professional bodies confirm the award through approved channels.

4

Document Forensics

Certificates and mark-sheets are checked with OCR and tamper detection where records are unavailable.

5

Equivalency & Report

Foreign awards are mapped to local frameworks and a per-credential report is issued with discrepancy status.
