Overview

What is Court Record Verification?

Quick answer

Court record verification searches civil and criminal court records, regulator orders and public enforcement registers for traces of litigation or offences attributable to the candidate. Verifitech searches jurisdiction by jurisdiction with strict name-and-identifier matching, and every finding is reported with case details, status and source.

Court records live in fragmented systems — eCourts and district courts in India, county and state courts in the US, the Courts Service in Ireland, state courts and police channels across the UAE and Singapore — and none of them cross-search automatically. Our checks combine those primary sources with regulator registers, insolvency records and global watchlists, then apply identity controls: date of birth, address history and identifier matching that keeps namesake hits off your report.

Court checks answer a different question than a police certificate: what is on the public record now, in civil as well as criminal matters. Pair them with police clearance verification for certificate-level evidence, global database verification for sanctions and watchlists, and ID verification to anchor the identity being searched. Know the difference

Professional Court Searches vs Name-Only Database Sweeps

A name typed into a search box is not a court check — here is what separates a defensible search from a false accusation.

AspectName-only database sweepsVerifitech court record verification
Identity controls Name matches return namesakes as “hits” — risky under GDPR and FCRA. DOB, address history and identifier matching separate the candidate from namesakes before anything is reported.
Jurisdiction coverage One national database, missing the courts where cases actually sit. County, district and national courts plus regulator registers, chosen from the candidate’s address and work history.
Case handling Raw hit lists with no context. Case number, court, charge or claim, filing date and current status — reported with source links.
Compliance posture Unclear lawful basis and retention. Consent-first, spent-conviction rules and adverse-action support applied per market.

Verifitech treats a “possible match” as an investigation, not a verdict — talk to our team about your jurisdiction matrix.
