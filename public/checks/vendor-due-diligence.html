Overview

What is Vendor Due Diligence?

Quick answer

Vendor due diligence verifies a supplier, partner or contractor as a legal entity and its people — registration and standing, beneficial ownership, directors and principals, sanctions and enforcement exposure, litigation and financial signals — before contracts are signed and periodically after. Verifitech builds it from primary registries and returns an audit-ready pack.

Third-party failures — shell vendors, sanctioned owners, phantom suppliers — cost far more than the diligence that prevents them. Verifitech verifies each vendor from primary sources: MCA and GST records in India, Secretary of State registries and SAM.gov in the US, Companies House in the UK, ACRA in Singapore, DED and free-zone licences in the UAE, and the CRO in Ireland — then screens the entity and its beneficial owners against sanctions and enforcement lists.

Vendor diligence is entity-level screening with people at its core: pair it with directorship verification and DIN verification for the principals, global database verification for sanctions, and credit checks where financial standing matters.
