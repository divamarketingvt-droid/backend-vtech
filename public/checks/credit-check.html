Overview

What is Credit Check?

Quick answer

A credit check reviews a person’s credit report — score, payment history, defaults, judgments and insolvencies — from a licensed credit bureau, used for roles handling money or credit, tenancy decisions and licensing. Verifitech runs every pull with documented consent and returns a decision-ready summary, not a raw dump.

Credit checks are permitted-use instruments: the bureau, the purpose and the candidate’s consent all have to line up before a report can be pulled. Verifitech handles that compliance plumbing per market — TransUnion CIBIL and Equifax in India, the three US bureaus under FCRA, AECB in the UAE, Credit Bureau Singapore and the Central Credit Register in Ireland — and applies the local rules on what may be used in employment decisions.

Credit checks usually sit in a wider pack: ID verification to anchor identity, court record checks for litigation beyond the credit file, and employment verification for the income story. See background verification companies in India for the programme view.

How Credit Checks Work

From consent to a decision-ready report — compliance built into every step.

The request routes to the right bureau for the market — CIBIL, the US three, AECB, CBS or the CCR.

The bureau report is analysed into score bands, adverse items and fraud signals relevant to your decision.

Analysts review adverse findings for accuracy and context before anything is flagged to you.

5

Decision-Ready Report
