Overview

What is DIN Verification?

Quick answer

DIN verification validates a Director Identification Number (or DPIN) against the Ministry of Corporate Affairs registry in India — confirming the person exists as a director, their status, any disqualifications and the companies they are associated with. Verifitech extends the same diligence to director registries in other markets.

A DIN is the eight-digit identity of a director in India’s corporate system — every company filing links to it, and disqualification orders attach to it. Verifying the DIN and its trail confirms three things at once: the person is who they claim, they are legally permitted to act as a director, and the companies they name (or omit) match the registry. That makes the check standard diligence for board appointments, vendor onboarding, KYC of directors and investment decisions.

For full board-level diligence, DIN checks pair with directorship verification for the complete appointment history, global database verification for sanctions and enforcement hits, and ID verification to anchor the individual. Vendor programmes add vendor due diligence on the entity itself.

How DIN Verification Works

From claimed identity to a registry-backed diligence pack.

1

Director Details Captured

Name, DIN (if known), date of birth and declared companies are captured with the requester’s basis for the check.

2

Registry Search

The MCA registry is searched — by DIN where available, by name and identifiers where not — to locate the director record.

3

Association Mapping

All companies and LLPs linked to the DIN are extracted with CIN, role and current status.

4

Disqualification Sweep

Disqualification and debarment registers are checked, with cross-market registries added where the career spans borders.

5

Diligence Report

A dated, registry-backed report is issued with extracts attached and every flag explained.
