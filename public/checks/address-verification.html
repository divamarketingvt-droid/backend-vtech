Overview

What is Address Verification?

Quick answer

Address verification confirms that the address a candidate or customer declares is real, current and associated with them — through digital document checks, credit-header and utility database matches, and physical site visits with geo-tagged photo proof. Verifitech combines the layers that fit each market and returns an audit-ready report.

An address failure looks harmless until it is the reason a court notice, a card or a regulator letter goes missing — or the signal behind a synthetic identity. Our checks layer the evidence: Aadhaar-linked address details and utility records in India, credit-header address history in the US, Emirates ID and Ejari tenancy data in the UAE, NRIC-linked records in Singapore and Revenue or RTB documents in Ireland. Documents are checked with OCR and tamper detection; where stakes are high, a field executive confirms the address physically.

Address checks usually run alongside the rest of the stack: ID verification to anchor identity, employment verification for the work history and passport verification for international hires. For the programme view, see our guide to background verification companies in India.

Know the difference

Digital + Field Verification vs Paper-Proof Collection

A utility bill in an inbox is not an address check — here is how the approaches compare.

Process

How Address Verification Works

From declared address to geo-tagged evidence — a transparent, consented flow.

1

Address Declaration

The candidate submits the current and permanent addresses with consent through an encrypted portal.

2

Digital Evidence Layer

Databases and documents are checked first — credit-headers, ID-linked addresses, utility bills and tenancy records.

3

Risk-Based Escalation

If digital evidence is thin or the role is high-risk, the check routes to a physical site visit.

4

Field Confirmation

Trained executives conduct the geo-tagged visit with photo proof, resident interaction and neighbourhood notes.

5

Consolidated Report

All evidence layers are compiled into one report with a clear verified, mismatched or unverifiable verdict.
