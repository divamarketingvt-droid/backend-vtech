How It Works

5-Step Digital Verification Process

From initiation to a tamper-proof report — our digital address verification is designed for speed without sacrificing accuracy.

1

Case Initiated

The employer submits the candidate's details through our dashboard or API, triggering the verification workflow instantly.

2

Candidate Notified

The candidate receives an SMS and email with a secure link to begin the self-service verification process on their mobile device.

3

ID & Residence Proof Submitted

The candidate submits their ID proof along with a photo of their residence — all captured with built-in validation.

4

Liveness & GPS Capture

A real-time liveness check confirms the candidate's identity while GPS coordinates are captured to validate the declared address location.

5

Report Generated

A tamper-proof report is generated instantly, complete with verification status, geo-data, and audit trail.

Trust & Compliance

Data Protection You Can Rely On

Address verification involves sensitive personal data — GPS coordinates, photographs, and government ID documents. Here's how we protect it.

Regulatory Compliance

Our address verification processes comply with the Information Technology Act 2000 and align with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 requirements for handling sensitive personal data.

Explicit Candidate Consent

GPS location capture, selfie liveness detection, and residence photography are only initiated after the candidate provides explicit, informed consent through a clear digital consent flow before any data collection begins.

Data Retention & Deletion

Candidate GPS coordinates, photographs, and ID document copies are retained for the duration required by the client's compliance policy, and automatically purged from our systems upon the agreed retention period expiry or upon client request.

Encryption Standards

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Access is restricted through role-based access controls with full audit trails.

Secure Infrastructure

Verification data is hosted on ISO 27001 certified infrastructure with regular penetration testing, vulnerability assessments, and continuous security monitoring.

Complete Audit Trail

Every action — from consent capture to report generation — is logged with timestamps and user identifiers, providing a complete chain of custody for regulatory audits.

FAQs — Digital Address Verification

Common questions about how our digital and physical address verification works, accuracy, data security, and turnaround times.

Every submission is validated using a combination of image analysis and geo-location checks. The system confirms the same individual is submitting the data and that the address aligns with the captured GPS coordinates.

All data is encrypted during transmission and storage using industry-standard security protocols. Strict access controls ensure only authorized personnel can access sensitive candidate information.

GPS tracking provides high precision, especially in urban areas. Multiple data points are used to validate the location and confirm the address closely matches the declared residence.

Digital verification is typically completed within minutes. Physical verification takes between 24 and 72 hours, depending on location and field executive availability.

Each report includes geo-tagged images, timestamps, and detailed verification notes. A secure validation mechanism ensures the report is genuine and tamper-proof.

For most hiring scenarios, verifying the current residential address is sufficient. For sensitive roles, verifying both current and permanent addresses provides stronger risk coverage.
