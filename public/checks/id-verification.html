Overview

What is ID Verification?

Quick answer

Identity verification (IDV) confirms that a government-issued document — a passport, national ID card, tax identity number or driving licence — is genuine, belongs to the person presenting it and is still valid and active. Verifitech runs the check in real time against the issuing authority and returns an instant, tamper-proof verification report — the foundation every later background check is built on.

Businesses use ID verification at the very first step of onboarding: pre-employment background screening, KYC for banks and fintechs, gig and delivery workforce activation, tenant and buyer onboarding in real estate, and student identity in education. Each document is validated against its own issuing source — UIDAI for Aadhaar and NSDL for PAN in India, ICP for the Emirates ID in the UAE, ICA for NRIC and FIN in Singapore, the SSA and state DMVs in the US, and DEASP and NDLS sources in Ireland — so a forged, borrowed or tampered document is caught before it enters your system.

There are two ways to verify. Real-time database verification checks the ID number directly against the issuing authority's records and returns status in seconds. Document + biometric verification captures the ID image, extracts the data with secure OCR, checks for tampering and matches a live selfie to the ID photo with liveness detection. Both produce an audit-ready report. Once identity is confirmed, layer the rest of the screening on top: employment verification, address verification, education verification and court record checks — see our guide to background verification companies in India for the full programme view.

ID Verification Checks by Country

Every country issues its own identity documents through its own authorities — so every country needs its own checks. Here is what we verify in the markets our clients hire from, and what each document actually means.

IN

India

Issuing sources: UIDAI, NSDL, Passport Seva (MEA), Election Commission of India & state transport authorities

  • AadhaarThe 12-digit biometric identity issued by UIDAI and the default eKYC proof in India — verified through authorized eKYC channels with OTP and demographic match. The number is masked, with only the last four digits stored.
  • PAN (Permanent Account Number)The 10-character tax identity from the Income Tax Department, issued via NSDL — confirms financial identity and flags duplicate or bogus PANs during hiring.
  • PassportIssued by the Ministry of External Affairs through Passport Seva — MRZ decoding and issuing-authority checks make it the strongest portable proof of identity for overseas hires.
  • Voter ID (EPIC)The Election Commission's photo identity card — confirms citizenship and supports address validation.
  • Driving LicenseIssued by state RTOs through Sarathi — confirms class, validity and holder details for gig, logistics and field-force roles.
US

United States

Issuing sources: Social Security Administration, state DMVs, Department of State & USCIS

  • Social Security Number (SSN)The 9-digit federal number tied to work and credit history — verified with the candidate's consent against name, date of birth and issuance records.
  • State Driver's LicenseIssued by state DMVs — format, expiry and status validation against issuing-state records.
  • US PassportThe Department of State travel document — MRZ validation plus document-level checks make it the strongest federal identity proof.
  • Permanent Resident Card (Green Card)USCIS-issued proof of lawful permanent residence — supports right-to-work confirmation.
  • State ID cardThe DMV-issued identity card for non-drivers — validated the same way as a license.
AE

United Arab Emirates

Issuing sources: ICP, GDRFA, MOHRE & emirate-level transport authorities (e.g. RTA Dubai)

  • Emirates IDThe mandatory national ID for every citizen and resident, issued by ICP — verification confirms validity, expiry and demographic match. The backbone of UAE onboarding.
  • UAE PassportThe citizen travel document — MRZ decoding and issuing-authority validation.
  • Residence visa & labour cardGDRFA/ICP residence permits and MOHRE labour cards confirm the holder's legal right to work in the UAE.
  • GCC national IDHome-country identity cards for GCC-national hires — checked for authenticity and holder match.
  • UAE Driving LicenseIssued by emirate-level authorities such as Dubai's RTA — class, expiry and status checks for drivers and riders.
SG

Singapore

Issuing sources: ICA, Ministry of Manpower (MOM) & the Traffic Police

  • NRICThe National Registration Identity Card for citizens and permanent residents, issued by ICA — format and checksum validation plus authorized record checks. The primary identifier for employment and CPF.
  • FINThe Foreign Identification Number assigned to foreigners on work passes — used when no NRIC exists.
  • Work passes (EP, S Pass, Work Permit)Ministry of Manpower documents confirming pass type, role and validity — Singapore's legal right-to-work check.
  • Singapore PassportICA-issued travel document with MRZ validation.
  • Singpass / MyInfoThe national digital identity — consented MyInfo pulls verify identity data straight against government records.
  • Driving LicenceIssued by the Traffic Police — class, status and expiry checks for driving roles.
IE

Ireland

Issuing sources: DEASP, Department of Foreign Affairs, NDLS/RSA & Immigration Service

  • PPS NumberThe Personal Public Service Number from the Department of Social Protection — Ireland's national identifier for payroll, tax and benefits, and the first check in Irish onboarding.
  • Irish PassportDepartment of Foreign Affairs travel document — MRZ validation.
  • Irish Driving LicenceIssued through the NDLS under the Road Safety Authority — category, expiry and status checks.
  • Irish Residence Permit (IRP)The immigration card for non-EEA nationals, formerly the GNIB card — confirms immigration status and right to work.
  • Public Services Card (PSC)The DEASP identity card used across public services — supports verified, auditable identity confirmation.

Know the difference

Automated ID Verification vs Manual Document Checks

The two approaches look similar on the surface — here is how they compare when the stakes are a hire, a loan or a licence.

AspectManual document reviewVerifitech automated ID verification
What it is A person visually reviews scanned or photocopied ID documents, often after onboarding has already started. A real-time check of the document and ID number against the issuing authority's own records, plus OCR extraction, tamper detection and biometric face match.
Turnaround Hours to days, depending on reviewer workload and document quality. Seconds for database checks; unclear or flagged cases are queued automatically for analyst review.
Fraud detection Depends on reviewer training — edited PDFs, borrowed IDs and deepfake photos often pass unnoticed. AI tamper detection, MRZ and format validation, liveness selfie match and authority cross-checks flag them automatically.
Output & audit trail A scanned copy sitting in an inbox — hard to evidence later in an audit or dispute. A tamper-proof verification report with every step logged, ready for auditors, regulators and courts.

Verifitech runs both layers together: automated checks for speed, with analyst review reserved for edge cases — talk to our team about the right mix for your onboarding flow.

Process

How ID Verification Works

From document upload to tamper-proof report — a fully automated, audit-ready flow.

1

Secure ID Submission

Candidates upload their government-issued ID — passport, national ID, Aadhaar, PAN, SSN card or driving licence — through an end-to-end encrypted portal or mobile app.

2

Intelligent Data Extraction

Secure OCR technology captures the key details from the document — name, number, date of birth, photograph — with high accuracy, eliminating manual entry errors.

3

Real-Time Database Validation

Extracted details are instantly verified against the issuing authority — UIDAI, NSDL, ICA, ICP, the SSA, DMVs and transport authorities — confirming the identity is valid, active and compliant.

4

Biometric Identity Matching

Facial recognition matches the candidate's live selfie with the ID photo, using liveness detection to eliminate impersonation and deepfake spoofing risks.

5

Instant Verification Report

A comprehensive report is generated in seconds — authenticity status, risk indicators and a tamper-proof certificate, ready to attach to the candidate's file.
