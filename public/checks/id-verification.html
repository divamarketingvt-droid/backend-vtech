The basics

What is identity verification?

Identity verification is the process of confirming that a government-issued ID — such as an Aadhaar card, PAN, passport, SSN, NRIC, Emirates ID or driving licence — is genuine, belongs to the person presenting it, and is still valid and active today. For employers, banks and onboarding teams in any market, it answers four questions before a candidate or customer is admitted: is the document real, does it belong to this person, is it valid and active, and can the check be proved later if audited.

Verifitech runs these checks electronically against issuing-authority sources — UIDAI for Aadhaar, NSDL for PAN, the UK DVLA and Home Office for licences and right to work, Australia's Document Verification Service, Singapore's ICA and MOM systems, and ICAO-standard passport checks worldwide — in real time. This replaces slow manual document review with an automated, auditable workflow that returns a verification report in seconds rather than days. It is the foundational step of any background verification program: before you check education, employment or credit, you first confirm the person is who they claim to be.

  • Does it belong to this person?
  • Is it valid and active today?
  • Can you prove the check later?

The process

How identity verification works

A seamless, secure five-step process that delivers accurate, fast and KYC-compliant identity verification — from document upload to a tamper-proof report, without friction for you or your candidates.

  1. Secure ID submission

    Candidates upload their government-issued ID — a national ID, passport, driving licence or residence permit — through an end-to-end encrypted web portal or mobile app, with data privacy built in from the very first step.

  2. Intelligent data extraction

    Secure OCR technology captures the structured key details from the document — name, number, date of birth, photograph — with high accuracy, eliminating manual data entry errors and speeding up the process.

  3. Real-time database validation

    Extracted information is instantly verified against issuing-authority databases and registries — national ID authorities, transport agencies, passport systems and work-authorization services — ensuring the identity is valid, active and compliant.

  4. Biometric identity matching

    Facial recognition technology securely matches the candidate's live selfie with the ID photo, using liveness detection to eliminate impersonation and deepfake spoofing risks.

  5. Instant verification report

    A comprehensive report is generated in seconds — authenticity status, risk indicators and a tamper-proof verification certificate for audit readiness, ready to attach to the candidate's file.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about ID verification

Clear answers to the most common questions about global identity verification — covering country coverage, document types, compliance and data security.

Which countries do you cover for ID verification?

We cover the seven markets we operate in — India, the USA, UK, UAE, Australia, Singapore and Ireland — with issuing-authority database checks in each. Beyond these, machine-readable passports and national IDs are verified against ICAO Doc 9303 standards and global watchlists, so you can onboard talent almost anywhere. For screening that goes beyond identity — employment, education and court record checks across borders — combine this with our global background check service.

What are the main ID documents used for verification in India?

The five most requested are Aadhaar, PAN, passport, Voter ID (EPIC) and Driving License. Aadhaar supports instant OTP or biometric eKYC through UIDAI; PAN is validated through NSDL; passports are checked with MRZ decoding; and driving licences are verified against state RTO records.

How do you verify documents from the USA, UK, UAE, Australia or Singapore?

Each country has its own issuing authorities and we verify against them: US driver's licences against AAMVA standards, UK licences and Right to Work through DVLA and Home Office share codes, Emirates ID through ICA records, Australian documents through the DVS and VEVO, and Singapore NRIC and work passes through ICA and MOM systems.

How do you prevent fake or tampered document uploads?

We combine intelligent technology with real-time validation to detect fraud. Our system checks for signs of image manipulation, validates document alignment against government standards (such as fonts and security elements), cross-verifies details with trusted databases, and uses live selfie verification to ensure the person is physically present and not a spoof or replay.

What is the accuracy rate of OCR data extraction?

Our OCR technology is built for precision and consistency. Standard documents with structured formats — Aadhaar, PAN, NRIC and passports — achieve high accuracy. If an uploaded image or PDF is unclear, the system instantly prompts the user to re-upload it, so only clean, reliable data enters the verification process.

How is personal identity data protected?

Data security is built into every layer of the process. All sensitive information is end-to-end encrypted in storage and during transmission, with handling aligned to GDPR in Europe, PDPA in Singapore and India's DPDP Act. Access is controlled through role-based permissions and multi-factor authentication, so only authorized users can view data based on their role.

What happens if a government database is temporarily unavailable?

Our system is designed to handle such scenarios without disruption. It automatically retries verification and queues requests if needed. Once the service is restored, the verification is completed and you are notified, so your workflow continues without manual follow-up.

Is Aadhaar verification legally compliant for Indian KYC?

Yes. Aadhaar-based eKYC is compliant with regulations when implemented through authorized channels that follow UIDAI guidelines. To ensure privacy, the Aadhaar number is masked and only the last four digits are stored for reference, keeping you compliant while protecting user data.
