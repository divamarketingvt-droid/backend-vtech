The Process

How Background Verification Works

Understanding the steps involved helps you provide the right information at the right time, speeding up your onboarding.

View Detailed Steps
1

Initiation

Your employer initiates the check. You will receive an email/SMS with your Application ID.

2

Data Entry & Authorization

Log in to the portal to fill in your details (Education, Employment, Address) and submit authorization.

3

Verification

Our team verifies your documents with universities and previous employers. This may take 3-10 days.

4

Report Generation

Once all checks are complete, a final report is generated and shared directly with your employer.
