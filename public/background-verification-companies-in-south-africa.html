How long does background verification take in South Africa?

Identity checks are instant to 24 hours through Department of Home Affairs validation and AFIS fingerprint checks. Address checks take 24 to 72 hours, employment checks 3 to 10 business days, education checks 5 to 10 business days (including SAQA and Umalusi verification), the SAPS Police Clearance Certificate 1 to 5 business days (fingerprint-based via the Criminal Record Centre), and ITC credit / court record checks instant to 24 hours.

What documents are needed for background verification in South Africa?

Usually a South African ID card or green-barcoded ID book, South African passport, or driver's licence; education certificates, transcripts and the National Senior Certificate (matric); previous employment letters and payslips; and address proof. For a criminal record check, fingerprints are captured for AFIS matching against the SAPS Criminal Record Centre. The candidate also gives digital consent, which Verifitech captures through OTP, as required under POPIA.

Is background verification legal in South Africa?

Yes. South African employers can verify candidates with informed, documented consent under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA, Act 4 of 2013), overseen by the Information Regulator. Verifitech captures consent for every check and follows POPIA's lawful-processing, purpose-limitation and security-safeguard principles. Get your own legal advice on policies specific to your industry.
