Is background verification legal in Malaysia?

Yes. Malaysian employers can verify candidates with informed, documented consent under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709), administered by the Personal Data Protection Department (JPDP). Verifitech captures consent for every check and follows the Act's notice, consent and purpose-limitation principles. Get your own legal advice on policies specific to your industry.
