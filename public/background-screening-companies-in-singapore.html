PDPA, TAFEP & MOM: What Singapore Employers Must Get Right

Background screening in Singapore sits at the intersection of data-protection law, fair-hiring rules and foreign-manpower controls.

Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) governs how employers collect, use and disclose candidate data during screening. Employers must notify candidates of the purpose, obtain consent before checks begin and only collect information a reasonable person would consider appropriate — with financial penalties that can reach 10% of annual Singapore turnover or S$1 million, whichever is higher. Verifitech captures purpose-limited consent digitally and documents every step for audit.

Fair-hiring rules sit alongside the PDPA. TAFEP's Tripartite Guidelines require merit-based selection free of age, race, gender, religion or disability discrimination, and the Workplace Fairness Act passed in January 2025 (with its dispute-resolution bill in November 2025) formalises these rules once in force. When hiring foreigners, MOM's Fair Consideration Framework requires a MyCareersFuture job ad open for at least 14 consecutive days before an Employment Pass application, and new EP applications are scored under COMPASS. For financial institutions, MAS fit-and-proper criteria add credit, directorship and regulatory-reference checks.

  • PDPA consent: Notice, purpose limitation and documented consent before any check begins — captured once, reused across the package.
  • TAFEP fair hiring: Merit-based screening aligned with the Tripartite Guidelines today and the Workplace Fairness Act once in force.
  • MOM compliance: Work pass validity checks with consent, plus Fair Consideration Framework (14-day MyCareersFuture ad) and COMPASS documentation support.
  • MAS fit & proper: Credit bureau reports, ACRA BizFile directorship searches and regulatory references for financial-sector roles.
  • Retention discipline: Candidate data kept only as long as the stated purpose requires, then purged on schedule.
  • Audit trail: Every consent, check and decision logged for regulator, auditor and internal review.
