Why Canadian Employers Need One Verification Partner Across Provinces

Canadian hiring crosses provincial lines, and privacy law changes with them. One candidate can study in one province, work in another and live in a third — under PIPEDA federally and Quebec Law 25, Alberta PIPA or BC PIPA locally. A single partner with national reach keeps the whole check in one report instead of several vendors, with consent captured to the strictest applicable rule.

  • Pan-Canada address checks: field executives and digital checks across 850,000+ postal codes in all 10 provinces and 3 territories.
  • Provincial education records: direct follow-up with universities and colleges, plus provincial credential assessment bodies like WES, IQAS and ICAS.
  • One report, many sources: an address in one province, a degree from another, a previous employer abroad and an RCMP record check in a single tamper-proof report.
  • Police and RCMP checks through every jurisdiction a candidate has lived in, plus global checks in 25+ countries for new immigrants and returning Canadians.

How long does background verification take in Canada?

Identity checks are instant to 24 hours. Address checks take 24 to 72 hours, employment checks 3 to 10 business days, education checks 5 to 10 business days, police record checks 3 to 7 business days, and RCMP certified criminal record checks (fingerprint-based) 1 to 3 weeks.

What documents are needed for background verification in Canada?

Usually a government ID such as a Social Insurance Number (SIN), Canadian passport, provincial driver's licence or Permanent Resident (PR) card, education transcripts and diplomas, previous offer and Records of Employment (ROE), pay stubs and address proof. The candidate also gives digital consent, which Verifitech captures through OTP, as required under PIPEDA.

Is background verification legal in Canada?

Yes. Canadian employers can verify candidates with informed, documented consent under the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and provincial privacy laws such as Quebec Law 25, Alberta PIPA and BC PIPA. Verifitech captures consent for every check. Get your own legal advice on policies specific to your industry and province.
