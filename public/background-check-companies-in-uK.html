Most packages average 3 to 5 days. Emirates ID checks are instant, visa and labour contract checks take 24 to 48 hours, employment checks 3 to 7 days, education and MOFA attestation 5 to 7 business days and police clearance 3 to 5 business days.

Usually the candidate's Emirates ID and passport with residence visa page, education certificates with MOFA attestation where applicable, previous offer and experience letters, and address details. The candidate also gives digital consent, which Verifitech captures before checks begin.

Is background verification legal in the UAE?

Yes. Employers in the UAE can verify candidates with informed consent. Verifitech captures consent for every check and processes data in line with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, and MoHRE guidelines.
