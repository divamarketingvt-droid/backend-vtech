What background check laws apply in Dallas?

How long does a background check take in Dallas?

Most packages average 3 to 5 days. SSN traces are instant, county criminal searches take 1 to 3 days, federal searches 1 to 2 days, employment and education verification 2 to 5 business days and drug screening 1 to 3 days depending on the lab.

What does FCRA compliance mean for employers?

The Fair Credit Reporting Act requires a standalone disclosure and written authorization before a background check, a pre-adverse action notice with a copy of the report and the Summary of Rights, a reasonable waiting period and a final adverse action notice. Verifitech builds every step into the workflow and documents each one.

What is ban-the-box or fair chance hiring?

Fair chance laws delay criminal history inquiries until after a conditional offer. New York City, California, Illinois and Philadelphia have their own versions; Verifitech configures your workflow to the law that applies to each location and tracks changes monthly.

Which checks are included in a US screening package?

Typically an SSN trace, county criminal searches for every county of residence, history and study, state and federal criminal searches, sex offender registry checks, employment and education verification, professional license checks, MVR for driver roles and 5-panel or 10-panel drug screening.
