Why Employers Across Ireland Need One Verification Partner

Irish hiring crosses county borders and continents. A candidate may live in Kildare, work in Dublin's Silicon Docks, hold a degree from the University of Galway and have worked in London or Warsaw. One partner with GNVB-registered vetting bodies, DETE permit checks and global reach keeps every check inside a single report instead of several vendors.

  • Nationwide vetting & criminal checks: Garda vetting support through registered organisations, court record checks and police certificates for candidates who lived abroad, plus global watchlists.
  • Right-to-work & permit data points: DETE employment permits, Irish Residence Permit cards and Stamp permissions validated from one platform.
  • EU & overseas education follow-up: direct confirmation with Irish universities, QQI awarding bodies and institutions across the EU, the UK, India and beyond.
  • One report, many sources: an EEA degree, a previous employer in another EU country and a Dublin address combined in a single tamper-proof report.

How long does background verification take in Ireland?

Most packages average 3 to 5 days. What documents are needed for a background check in Ireland?

Usually the candidate's passport or Irish Residence Permit, education certificates, previous offer and experience letters, references and address details. The candidate also gives digital consent, which Verifitech captures before checks begin.

Is background verification legal in Ireland?

Yes. Employers in Ireland can verify candidates with informed consent under the EU GDPR and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018. Verifitech captures consent for every check and uses lawful channels such as vetting through GNVB-registered organisations, sworn declarations and overseas police certificates. Get your own legal advice on policies specific to your industry.
